# 🎛️ Serum 2 (Xfer Records)

Serum 2 is the second generation of one of the most popular synthesizers in electronic music. It is used for basses, leads, pads and sound effects in genres from EDM and dubstep to hip-hop and pop. Unlike the first version, it is not limited to a wavetable oscillator: it offers several types of sound engines, plus a built-in arpeggiator and clip sequencer.

## 📖 About

The dream synth seemed not to exist: a wavetable synthesizer with truly high-quality sound and a visual, creative, workflow-oriented interface that makes creating and changing sounds fun instead of tedious, with the ability to dig deeper when you want to, creating, importing, editing and transforming wavetables and manipulating them in real time during playback.

### Filters

Serum includes all the filter types found in LFOTool, along with some completely new ones: flangers, phasers and comb filters, each of which can track the musical note you are playing.

Dual filter types let you control the filter types or switch between them. Get creative with unconventional processes for filters, such as sample rate reduction, or with unique filter types that haven't appeared anywhere before, like a dirty-sounding LPF.

### Unison and stacking

Serum lets you stack a single oscillator to use up to 16 voices. Each wavetable oscillator has a range of advanced unison parameters.

Stack settings let you layer notes (octaves, for example) for a fuller sound from a single key press.

Set all unison voices to a unique waveform at once with the Unison WT Pos setting, or tune them all differently with the Unison Warp control. A variety of unison modes for each oscillator lets you get a blooming or swarming stacked sound, exactly as you want it.

## ✨ What's new in Serum 2

- Five oscillator types: wavetable, multisample, sample, granular, spectral
- Improved wavetable oscillator: smooth interpolation and dual warp modes
- Built-in arpeggiator (up to 32 steps)
- Clip sequencer for patterns inside the plugin
- Flexible effects routing
- Compatible with Serum 1 presets
- Large library of factory presets and wavetables
